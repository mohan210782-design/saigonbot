## 4. Services & Support

### 4.1 What is Robot-as-a-Service (RaaS)?

RaaS is a subscription model where you pay a monthly fee for robot deployment instead of purchasing hardware upfront. The fee covers the robot hardware, software, maintenance, support, and regular upgrades. This eliminates capital expenditure and reduces deployment risk. RaaS plans are available for all our products.

### 4.2 How much does a Chikku robot cost?

Pricing varies by model, configuration, and deployment scale. We also offer **Robot-as-a-Service (RaaS)** subscription options with no upfront capital expenditure. Contact our sales team for a customized quote.
